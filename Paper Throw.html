Paper Throw

Crush the report, toss it in the bin, hit your targets. Three levels, one stack of paperwork.

  • PickPress and hold on the paper pile. A sheet lifts into your hand.
  • CrushKeep holding. The sheet crumples into a ball and the power meter starts.
  • ThrowSlide under the bin and let go while the needle is in the green band.

Works with mouse or touch. Sound is on by default. Best score: 0

How to play

Hit the target number of sheets in each level before you run out of paper.

  • PickPress and hold on the paper pile.
  • CrushKeep holding until the sheet is a ball.
  • ThrowYour finger or cursor sets where the ball lands. Release when the needle is in the green band. On moving bins, aim ahead of them.
